ABOUT ATELIER GENÈSE

Every Brand
Has a Beginning.

Atelier Genèse is an independent creative studio focused on creating thoughtful visual beginnings for emerging and evolving brands.

Atelier Genèse 是一間獨立創意工作室， 專注於為正在誕生或成長中的品牌打造清晰、精緻且值得被記住的視覺起點。

THE NAME

Genèse means
beginning.

Genèse speaks to origin, birth and the moment something begins. We believe a brand identity should be more than something that looks good. It should become the visual foundation from which a brand can grow.

Genèse 象徵起源、誕生與開始。 我們相信品牌識別不應只停留在「好看」， 而應成為品牌未來成長時，可以持續延伸的視覺基礎。

OUR APPROACH

Thoughtful.
Refined.
Intentional.

Every project begins by understanding the brand before creating the visual result. We look for clarity, character and a direction that feels distinct to each client.

Rather than creating something simply to finish a project, Atelier Genèse values considered design that can remain meaningful as the brand develops.

每一個設計都從理解品牌開始。 我們重視的不只是完成作品，而是找到真正適合品牌的方向， 讓視覺能夠隨著品牌一起成長。
