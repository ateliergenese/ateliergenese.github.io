PROJECT INFORMATION

Before
We Begin.

A few things to know before starting a project with Atelier Genèse. Each collaboration is considered individually, allowing the process to respond to the needs of each brand.

在開始合作之前，這裡整理了一些你可能會想了解的資訊。 每個專案都會依品牌需求與實際設計內容個別規劃。

Fill out the project inquiry form with some basic information about your brand and design needs. After reviewing your request, we will contact you to discuss the project in more detail.

填寫專案詢問表單，簡單告訴我們你的品牌與設計需求。 收到資料後，我們會進一步與你聯繫，確認專案內容與合作方式。

Each project is quoted individually based on its scope, requirements and timeline. A final quote will be provided after we understand your project in more detail.

每個專案會依設計範圍、需求與時程個別報價。 在了解完整需求後，我們會提供正式報價。

Project timelines vary depending on the scope and complexity of the work. An estimated schedule will be discussed before the project begins.

專案時程會依設計內容與複雜程度而有所不同， 我們會在正式開始前與你確認預計時程。

Any information that helps us understand your brand is helpful, such as your brand story, target audience, preferred visual direction, references, existing materials and project goals.

任何能幫助我們了解品牌的資訊都可以提供， 例如品牌故事、目標客群、喜歡的視覺方向、參考圖片、 現有品牌素材與這次專案希望達成的目標。
